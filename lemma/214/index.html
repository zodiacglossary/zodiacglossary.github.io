nanmurtu ša rabê

IGI ša₂ ŠU₂
Akkadian · noun
Edited by: Mathieu Ossendrijver
Last edited: 2023-08-30
Meanings
1. Evening First
2. western appearance
Literal translation
western appearance
Categories
synodic phenomenon

ša₂ GU₄.UD IGI ša₂ ŠU₂ ana IGI ša₂ ŠU₂ TA 6 ALLA EN 26 RIN₂ 2.40 TAB

‘For Mercury: western appearance to western appearance from 6 Cancer until 26 Libra you add 2,40.’

— AO 6477 (BMAPT No. 42) obv. 11

Genre: mathematical astronomy: procedure text Provenance: Uruk Date: ca. 200 BCE
