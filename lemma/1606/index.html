Variants
pedes leonis
leonis pedes

1. the feet of the Lion

1.a the feet of the Lion animal part

15°–17° Leo

tres in pedibus,

‘the three [following degrees] are [located] in the feet [of the Lion]’

— Firmicus Maternus, Mathesis 8.4.5

Genre: astrological compendium Provenance: Sicily Date: ca. 334 CE

Firmicus Maternus. 1897. Ivlii Firmici Materni Matheseos Libri VIII, ed. W. Kroll, F. Skutsch. Stuttgart: B. G. Teubner. 2.289
