bīt niṣirti

E₂ niṣirti
Akkadian · noun
variants E₂ ni-ṣir-tu₄ : bīt niṣirtu , E₂ ni-ṣir-ti₃ : bīt niṣirti , E₂ ni-ṣir-tu₂ : bīt niṣirtu
Edited by: Alessia Pilloni
Last edited: 2024-12-16
Meanings
1. exaltation
2. planet assigned to the triplicity of the month of the solstice/equinox that is the closest to the date of birth
3. planet assigned to the “Terms” of the month of birth.
Literal translation
house of secrecy/protection
Categories
astrological concept
astrological position
planetary position
astrological place
Related Lemmata 11 ›

1. exaltation

1.a exaltation astrological concept, astrological position, planetary position

said of planets, placed in a zodiacal constellation

šu-pul me-e mu-ú-tú [: š]u-pul « šá » AN-e šú-u : ⸢É⸣ ni-ṣir-tú šá dṢal-bat-a-nu

‘The depth of the Waters of Death is the depth of the sky, which refers to the exaltation of Mars.’

— BM 47529 + 47685 17

Genre: commentary Provenance: Babylon or Borsippa Date: Neo-Babylonian

Wee, J. Z., 2016. A Late Babylonian Astral Commentary on Marduk's Address to the Demons, Journal of Near Eastern Studies 75. 136-137

2. planet assigned to the triplicity of the month of the solstice/equinox that is the closest to the date of birth

2.a planet assigned to the triplicity of the month of the solstice/equinox that is the closest to the date of birth astrological concept, astrological place

only in horoscopes (Pilloni 2024)

MU.BU ITI.AB 8 šamaš ina E2 ni-ṣir-tu4 šá dil-bat LU2.TUR a-lid

‘That year, the 8th of Ṭebētu (Month X) was the date of the (winter) solstice. The child is born in the house of secrecy of Venus.’

— BM 36943 (=BH 8) rev. 1-3

Genre: horoscope Provenance: Babylon Date: 3rd century BCE

Rochberg, F. 1998. Babylonian Horoscopes. Transactions of the American Philosophical Society, 88, 1–164. 76-78

Pilloni, A. (2024). The Astrological Schemes Behind bīt niṣirtu and KI in the Babylonian Horoscopes. Journal of Ancient Near Eastern History, 11(1), 1-26. https://doi.org/10.1515/janeh-2023-0014

3.a planet assigned to the “Terms” of the month of birth. astrological concept, astrological position, astrological place

only in horoscopes (Pilloni 2024)
