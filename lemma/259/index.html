1. time between last moonrise before sunset and sunset

1.a time between last moonrise before sunset and sunset astronomical quantity: lunar six interval

GI₆ 13 10.30 ME muš

‘Night of (day) 13, ME = 10;30, measured.’

— BM 41616+ (ADRT II No. -255) obv. 5

Genre: astronomical diary Provenance: Babylon Date: 256 BCE

Sachs, A. J., Hunger, H. 1989. Astronomical Diaries and Related Texts from Babylonia, Vol. II: Diaries from 261 B.C. to 165 B.C. Vienna: Verlag der Österreichischen Akademie der Wissenschaften. 18-19
