radiatione convenio

Latin verb

Last edited: 2023-12-12
Primary meaning to be in aspect
Literal translation to come together with radiation
Variants
radiatione convenio
convenio radiatione
Edited by
Kierán Meinhardt
to be in aspect astrological concept

Si Lunam de diametro Mars et Saturnus pariter aspexerint, et nulla benivolarum stellarum vel Lunam vel illos qui sunt in diametro constituti, salutari radiatione convenerit, aut servos efficiet ista coniunctio, aut privates parentum praesidio misero faciet orbitatis onere praegravari.

‘If Mars and Saturn are equally in opposition to the moon and no benefic aspects either the Moon or the planets opposite her with benefic radiation, this configuration either makes slaves or orphans that are burdened with bereavement’

— Firmicus Maternus, Mathesis 6.29.3

Genre: astrological compendium
Provenance: Sicily
Date: ca. 334 CE

Firmicus Maternus. 1897. Ivlii Firmici Materni Matheseos Libri VIII, ed. W. Kroll, F. Skutsch. Stuttgart: B. G. Teubner. 2.133
