radiis orno

Latin verb

Last edited: 2023-12-12
Primary meaning to be in favorable aspect
Literal translation to adorn with rays
Variants
radiis orno
orno radiis
Edited by
Kierán Meinhardt
to be in favorable aspect astrological concept

Respice etiam quatenus hoc idem signum benivolarum stellarum radiia ornetur.

‘Also look at how the same sign is aspected by the benefics’

— Firmicus Maternus, Mathesis 6.32.3

Genre: astrological compendium
Provenance: Sicily
Date: ca. 334 CE

Firmicus Maternus. 1897. Ivlii Firmici Materni Matheseos Libri VIII, ed. W. Kroll, F. Skutsch. Stuttgart: B. G. Teubner. 2.175
