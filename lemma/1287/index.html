1. east

1.a east cardinal direction

ina 30 MU.MEŠ 7.20 KI-šu₂ ana GIŠ.NIM DIB

‘In 30 years its (Saturn's) position passes 7;20 to the east.’

— BM 41004 rev. 15

Genre: mathematical astronomy: procedure text Provenance: Babylon
