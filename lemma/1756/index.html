1. to divide

1.a to divide mathematical operation, arithmetical operation

ỉw=w ḏd n=k [fy 15 2/3 r 100] | ỉw=k šꜥṱ 6 ẖ[n 100 sp 94] | iw=k ḏd ḫr-š[m 6 r 94 r1]5 2/3 | my ỉn=f 1/3 r 47

‘If it is said to you [carry 15 2/3 to 100,] | you subtract 6 from [100, 94 remains]. | You shall say [6] goes to 94 with the result 1]5 2/3 | Cause it to be brought to 1/3 result 47.’

— P.Cairo J.E. 89127 1-4

Genre: mathematical text Provenance: Tuna el-Gebel Date: 3rd cent. BCE

R.A. Parker, 1972, Demotic Mathematical Texts, Brown Egyptological Studies 7, Providence RI/London. 37
