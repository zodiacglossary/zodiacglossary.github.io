मेष
meṣa

Sanskrit noun

Last edited: 2025-09-30
Primary meaning Aries
Literal translation ram, sheep
Edited by
Aries zodiac sign

आद्यः स्मृतो मेषसमानमूर्तिः कालस्य मूर्धा गदितः पुराणैः।

ādyaḥ smṛto meṣasamānamūrtiḥ kālasya mūrdhā gaditaḥ purāṇaiḥ|

‘The first [sign] (Aries), known to have the form similar to a ram, is said by the ancients to be the head of Time (kāla).’

— Vṛddhayavanajātaka 1.4ab

Genre: astrological treatise
Provenance: India
Date: ca. 300–325 CE

Mak, Bill M. 2018. “The First Two Chapters of Mīnarāja’s Vṛddhayavanajātaka.” Zinbun 48: 1–31. 15
