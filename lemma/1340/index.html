aspicio

Latin verb

Last edited: 2023-12-01
Primary meaning to be in aspect
Literal translation to look at
Variants
aspicio
Edited by
Kierán Meinhardt
to be in aspect astrological concept

Quodsi simul Mercurium ac Venerem collocates malignum sidus Martis aspexerit, faciles nuptias sed cum quadam infamia ac nota decernunt.

‘If Mercury and Venus are in conjunction and the malefic planet Mars aspects them, they decree easy but scandalous marriages’

— Firmicus Maternus, Mathesis 6.26.3

Genre: astrological compendium
Provenance: Sicily
Date: ca. 334 CE

Firmicus Maternus. 1897. Ivlii Firmici Materni Matheseos Libri VIII, ed. W. Kroll, F. Skutsch. Stuttgart: B. G. Teubner. 2.130
