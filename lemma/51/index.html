1. Aries

1.a Aries zodiac sign

ỉbd 2 pr.t pꜣ ỉsw

‘Month 2 of Peret (Mecheir): Aries’

— O.Strasb.Dem. 521 7

Genre: astronomical list Provenance: Egypt Date: Roman

Neugebauer, O., 1943. Demotic Horoscopes, Journal of the American Oriental Society 63, 121–122. 121
