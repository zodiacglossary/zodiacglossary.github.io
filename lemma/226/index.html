Egyptian noun

Edited by: Andreas Winkler

Literal translation
eastern setting

1. morning last

1.a morning last synodic phenomenon

pꜣy=f ḥtp ỉꜣbṱ 1223 tꜣy=f rnp.t ꜥꜣ.t 388

‘Its eastern setting 1223; its great year 388’

— ANAshm.D.O. 525+ 9

Genre: mathematical astronomy: procedure text Provenance: Egypt
