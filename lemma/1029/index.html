radiatione pulsor

Latin verb

Last edited: 2023-12-12
Primary meaning to be in aspect
Literal translation to be hit by radiation
Variants
radiatione pulsor
Edited by
Kierán Meinhardt
to be in aspect astrological concept

in Occasu posita, violenta Martis radiatione pulsetur.

‘located in the descendant, it is badly aspected with Mars’

— Firmicus Maternus, Mathesis 2.29.17

Genre: astrological compendium
Provenance: Sicily
Date: ca. 334 CE

Firmicus Maternus. 1897. Ivlii Firmici Materni Matheseos Libri VIII, ed. W. Kroll, F. Skutsch. Stuttgart: B. G. Teubner. 1.84
