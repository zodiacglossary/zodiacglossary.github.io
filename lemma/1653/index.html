𓎡𓈖𓅓𓏏𓇼
knm.t

Egyptian noun

Last edited: 2024-10-28
Primary meaning Decan 2 (Senmut; Seti I A; Seti I C ; Ramses VI E); Decan 3 (Seti I B); Decan 1 (Tanis);
Literal translation a mama, presumably an enemy of monkeys

𓎡𓈖𓅓𓏏𓇼

knm.t

‘a mama, presumably an enemy of monkeys’

— Neugebauer

Genre: tombs and temples
Provenance: Thebes; Roda, Hermopolis, Abydos, Esna, Edfu, Philae, Dendera, Tanis
Date: ca. 1200 BCE

Neugebauer, O. & Parker, R.A. EAT 3 106, 119, 129, 135, 141, 150
