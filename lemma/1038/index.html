MURUB₄ GE₆
qabal mūši

Akkadian noun

Last edited: 2024-11-03
Edited by: Marvin Schreiber
See also
〈EN.NUN.MURUB₄.BA〉 qablītu ‘Middle watch of the night’
Meanings
1. middle watch of the night
Categories
time interval
Literal translation
middle of the night
Variants
MURUB4 GE6 qabal mūši

1. middle watch of the night

1.a middle watch of the night time interval

AB 1 GE6 9 MURUB4 G[E6] |lu2.TUR a-l[id]

‘Month X 1, night of the 9th, middle of nig[ht,] | the child was bo[rn].’

— BM 34003 (LBAT 1470) (BH 23) 2-3

Genre: horoscope Provenance: Babylon Date: 1st century BCE (date of birth in horoscope: S.E. 223 X.9 = -87 Jan.5)

Rochberg, F. 1998. Babylonian Horoscopes. Transactions of the American Philosophical Society, 88. 127
