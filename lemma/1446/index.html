τετράγωνος πλευρά
tetragōnos pleura

Greek noun

Last edited: 2025-01-30
Edited by: Michael Zellmann-Rohrer
See also
〈πλευρά〉 pleura ‘side’
〈τετράγωνον〉 tetragōnon ‘square’
Meanings
1. side of square
Categories
astrological concept
aspect
Literal translation
square side
Variants
τετράγωνος πλευρά tetragōnos pleura

1. side of square

1.a side of square astrological concept, aspect

ζητήσαντες καθὼς προεκτεθείμεθα εὕρομεν τὴν ἐναντίωσιν Ἄρεως καὶ Ἀφροδίτης χρηματίζουσαν οὕτως κατὰ τὸ ζʹ κλίμα καὶ τὴν τετράγωνον πλευρὰν Κρόνου

‘By searching in the manner in which we have set out, we found the opposition of Mars and Venus thus operative according to the 7th clime, and the square side (i.e., quadrangle) of Saturn’

— Vettius Valens 7.6.30

Genre: astrological treatise Date: 2nd century CE

(GH L 124)
