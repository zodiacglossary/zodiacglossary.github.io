Ἐπαγόμεναι
Epagomenai

Greek noun

Last edited: 2024-12-09
Primary meaning Epagomenai
Literal translation Epagomenai
Variants
Ἐπαγόμεναι Epagomenai
Ἐπαγό Epago abbreviation
Ἐπα Epa abbreviation
Edited by
Thomas Peeters
Epagomenai month, time interval

month XIII (intercalary month) in the Alexandrian and Egyptian calendar; shortened from ἐπαγόμεναι ἡμέραι "intercalary days"

...]εταιτη | | Ἐπ[αγομ]ένων Πάρθενος | [...] | [...] ιαʹ 𐆊 μʹ | [...

‘...]? | | Epagomenai in Virgo | [...] | [...] 11 0 40 | [...’

— P. Oxy. LXI 4204 15

Genre: mathematical astronomy: almanac
Provenance: Oxyrhynchus
Date: after 54 CE

Jones, A. (ed.), 1999. Astronomical papyri from Oxyrhynchus (P. Oxy. 4133-4300a), Volumes I and II. Philadelphia: American Philosophical Society. I: 218-219 + II: 288-293

Read Παρθένῳ for Πάρθενος.

...] Ἐπαγό [...

‘...] Epago(menai) [...’

— P. Oxy. LXI 4208 11

Genre: mathematical astronomy: five-day almanac
Provenance: Oxyrhynchus
Date: after 296 CE

Jones, A. (ed.), 1999. Astronomical papyri from Oxyrhynchus (P. Oxy. 4133-4300a), Volumes I and II. Philadelphia: American Philosophical Society. I: 222 + II: 304-305
