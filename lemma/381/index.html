šur narkabti ša iltāni
ŠUR GIGIR ša₂ SI

Akkadian noun

Last edited: 2024-10-27
Edited by: Mathieu Ossendrijver
See also
〈šur narkabti ša šūti〉 šur GIGIR ša₂ ULU₃ ‘ζ Tauri’
Meanings
1. β Tauri
Categories
star
normal star
Literal translation
the northern rein of the chariot
Variants
šur GIGIR ša₂ SI šur narkabti ša iltāni
šur SI šur iltāni

1. β Tauri

1.a β Tauri star, normal star

Jones (2004) c6

15 ina ZALAG₂ sin⸣ ina IGI šur GIGIR ša₂ SI 2 ⸢KUŠ₃⸣

‘Night of (day) 15, last part of the night, Sin was 2 cubits in front of the northern rein of the chariot.’

— BM 34792 (ADRT I No. -373B) obv. 3'

Genre: astronomical diary Provenance: Babylon Date: 374 BCE

Sachs, A. J., Hunger, H. 1988. Astronomical Diaries and Related Texts from Babylonia, Vol. I: Diaries from 652 B.C. to 262 B.C. Vienna: Verlag der Österreichischen Akademie der Wissenschaften. 104-105

⸢USAN⸣ MUL₂.BABBAR SIG šur SI 2 1/2 KUŠ₃

‘First part of the night, Jupiter was 2 1/2 cubits below the northern rein (of the chariot).’

— BM 45722+ (ADRT II No. -182C) obv. 17

Genre: astronomical diary Provenance: Babylon Date: 183 BCE

Sachs, A. J., Hunger, H. 1989. Astronomical Diaries and Related Texts from Babylonia, Vol. II: Diaries from 261 B.C. to 165 B.C. Vienna: Verlag der Österreichischen Akademie der Wissenschaften.
