mi-ḫir miḫru
GABA.RI miḫru

1. counterpart

1.a counterpart astrological concept

šum4-ma GU4.UD u GENNA ina KI.GUB lu ina DUR lu ina mi-ḫir GUB-iz LU2 BI NU DAB

‘If Mercury of Saturn stand in a stationary position or in the DUR or in the opposite: this man will not be seized.’

— BM 34618 (LBAT 1596) 13-14

Genre: astrological medicine Provenance: Babylon Date: 4th-1st century BCE

Hunger, H., 2014. A Late Babylonian Astrological Tablet. In: Studies in Economic and Social History of the Ancient Near East in Memory of Péter Vargyas, ed. Zoltán Csabai and Tibor Grüll. Budapest: L'Harmattan: 657-669 658
