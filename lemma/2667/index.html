ἀναβαίνω

anabainō
Greek · verb
Edited by: Kierán Meinhardt
Last edited: 2024-12-12
Meanings
1. to ascend
Literal translation
to go up
Categories
motion
Related Lemmata 3 ›

1. to ascend

1.a to ascend motion

φοβʹ // συνῆπτεν ἡ σελή[νη ... ἀναβαίνουσα τὰ βόρι[α ... λοιπῶν ἀστέρων [...

‘572 // The moon reached [... ascending the north [... the rest of the stars [...’

— P. Oxy. LXI 4140 i-ii, 6-8

Genre: astronomical list Provenance: Oxyrhynchus Date: 2nd century CE

Jones, A. (ed.), 1999. Astronomical papyri from Oxyrhynchus (P. Oxy. 4133-4300a), Volumes I and II. Philadelphia: American Philosophical Society. I: 100-101 + II: 24-25
