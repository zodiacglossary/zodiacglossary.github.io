pḥ.wy ḏꜣ.t

Egyptian noun

Last edited: 2024-11-11
Primary meaning Decan 5 (Senmut; Seti I A; Seti I C; Tanis; Ramses VI); Decan 6 (Seti I B)
Literal translation The end of Djat

Decan 5 (Senmut; Seti I A; Seti I C; Tanis; Ramses VI) decan

𓄖𓏭𓍑𓄿𓏏𓇼

pH.wy DA.t

‘The end of Djat’

— Neugebauer

Genre: tombs and temples
Provenance: Thebes; Roda, Hermopolis, Abydos, Esna, Edfu, Philae, Dendera, Tanis
Date: ca. 1200 BCE

Neugebauer, O. & Parker, R.A. EAT 3 107, 120, 129, 135, 142, 150

Decan 6 (Seti I B) decan
Decan 3 of Leo

Firmicus Maternus; Sethi IB
