τριακονθήμερος

triakonthēmeros
Greek · adjective

Last edited: 2024-11-25

1. of thirty days

1. of thirty days

1.a of thirty days attribute of duration

Καὶ πάντα μὲν τὰ ζῴδια τριακονταμόριά ἐστιν, οὐ πάντα δὲ τριακονθήμερα.

‘Also, all zodiac signs consist of thirty degrees, but not all of thirty days.’

— Geminus 1.8

Genre: astronomy Date: 1st century BCE

Geminus. 1975. Géminos, Introduction aux Phénomènes, ed. G. Aujac. Paris: Les Belles Lettres. 2
