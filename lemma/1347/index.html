testimonium commodo

Latin verb

Last edited: 2023-12-12
Primary meaning to be in favorable aspect
Literal translation to put testimony in order
Variants
testimonium commodo
commodo testimonium
Edited by
Kierán Meinhardt
to be in favorable aspect astrological concept

Quodsi sic malivolis sicut diximus constitutis horoscopo Venus aut luppiter testimonium conmodaverint, expositus colligetur et alienae misericordiae praesidio nutrietur.

‘If as we said Venus and Jupiter aspect the malefic planets on the ascendant favorably, the exposed infant will be picked up and nourished by a stranger’

— Firmicus Maternus, Mathesis 6.29.6

Genre: astrological compendium
Provenance: Sicily
Date: ca. 334 CE

Firmicus Maternus. 1897. Ivlii Firmici Materni Matheseos Libri VIII, ed. W. Kroll, F. Skutsch. Stuttgart: B. G. Teubner. 2.134
