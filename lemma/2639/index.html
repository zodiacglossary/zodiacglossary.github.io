μῆκος
mēkos

Greek noun

Last edited: 2025-09-09
Primary meaning longitude
Literal translation length
Variants
μῆκος mēkos
Edited by
Thomas Peeters
Kierán Meinhardt
longitude astronomical term, astronomical quantity, spatial concept

Στίλβων ὁ τοῦ Ἑρμο[ῦ] ἀστ[ήρ, ὁ] τὴ[ν] ζώ- νην τοῦ ἀέρος λελογχώς εὑρέθη [τὴ]ν κα- τὰ μῆκος κείνησιν ποιούμενος ἐ[ν] ζῳδίῳ [...] Αἰγόκερῳ

‘Stilbon, the star of Mercury, which has been allotted the belt of the air, has been found making its longitudinal motion in the [...] sign Capricorn’

— P. Oxy. LXI 4277 i. 2-5

Genre: horoscope
Provenance: Oxyrhynchus

Jones, A. (ed.), 1999. Astronomical papyri from Oxyrhynchus (P. Oxy. 4133-4300a), Volumes I and II. Philadelphia: American Philosophical Society. II: 420-421

right ascension spatial concept, astronomical term

Ὁ Στέφανος ἐν τῷ βορείῳ ἡμισφαιρίῳ κείμενος κατὰ μῆκος μὲν ἐπέχει μο θʹ καὶ δʹʹ ἀπὸ τῆς αʹ μο τοῦ Σκορπίου ἕως ιʹ <καὶ> δʹʹ μο τοῦ αὐτοῦ ζῳδίου. Κατὰ πλάτος δ’ ἐπέχει μο ϛʹ 𐅵 καὶ δʹ ἀπὸ μθʹ μο ἀπὸ τοῦ βορείου πόλου ἕως μο νεʹ 𐅵 καὶ δʹ.

‘Corona Borealis, lying in the northern hemisphere, as for length spans 9 deg and ¼ from the 1st deg of Scorpio until 10 <and> ¼ deg of the same zodiac sign. And as for breadth, it spans 6, ½ and ¼ deg from 49 deg from the North Pole until 55, ½ and ¼ deg.’

— Codex Climaci Rescriptus 48r

Genre: star list

Gysembergh, V., Williams, P.J. and Zingg, E., 2022. New evidence for Hipparchus’ Star Catalogue revealed by multispectral imaging, Journal for the History of Astronomy 53 (4): 383-393. 386

Note that “6, ½ and ¼” is used to indicate “6 and ¾”, and similarly “55, ½ and ¼” is used to indicate “55 and ¾”. The text systematically uses an abbreviation μο (with the ο written above the μ) of μοῖρα to indicate degrees, here translated as deg.
