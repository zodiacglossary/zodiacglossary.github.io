IGI.DU₈.A.AN
tāmartu

Akkadian noun

Last edited: 2023-10-30
Edited by: Marvin Schreiber
See also
〈IGI〉 nanmurtu ‘first appearance’
Meanings
1. appearance
2. spectacle, sight, viewing
Categories
visual perception
synodic phenomenon
astronomical phenomenon
Literal translation
appearance
Variants
IGI.DU₈.A.AN tāmartu
IGI.DU₈.A tāmartu
IGI.LAL tāmartu

1. appearance

1.a appearance visual perception, synodic phenomenon, astronomical phenomenon

ina IGI.DU8.A.AN ša2 d.GU4.UD ina 3 u4-me ŠUR-nun

‘At the appearance of Mercury it will rain for 13 days.’

— AO 6449 (TCL VI 19 = TU 19) obv. 7

Genre: astrological weather prediction Provenance: Uruk Date: 13 August 226 BCE (Seleucid Era year 84, 12 V)

Hunger, H. 1976. Astrologische Wettervorhersagen. Zeitschrift fur Assyriologie 66. 247

2. spectacle, sight, viewing

2.a spectacle, sight, viewing
