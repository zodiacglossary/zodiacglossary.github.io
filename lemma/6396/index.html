त्रित्रिकोण
tritrikoṇa

Sanskrit noun

Last edited: 2026-01-06
Primary meaning 9th House
Literal translation threefold triangle
Edited by
Poorvaa Malhotra
9th astrological house in trine

ādyāccaturthaṃ caturaśramāhustathāṣṭamaṃ naidhanamapyuśanti | pṛthaktrikoṇaṃ tvatha paṃcamaṃ hi syāt tritrikoṇaṃ navamaṃ ca vindyāt ||51||

आद्याच्चतुर्थं चतुरश्रमाहुस्तथाष्टमं नैधनमप्युशन्ति । पृथक्त्रिकोणं त्वथ पंचमं हि स्यात् त्रित्रिकोणं नवमं च विन्द्यात् ॥५१॥

‘From the ascendant, the fourth house is called the Chaturasra (square); the eighth is also known as Naidhana (related to death). But individually the fifth house is called Trikoṇa (triangle), and the ninth is recognized as Tri-trikoṇa (the great or triple triangle)’

— Yavanajātaka v.1.51

Genre: astrological treatise
Provenance: India
Date: 4th-6th century CE

Pingree, David. The Yavanajātaka of Sphujidhvaja. Harvard Oriental Series, vol. 48. Cambridge: Harvard University Press 52
