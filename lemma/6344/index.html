सिंह
siṃha

Sanskrit noun

Last edited: 2025-09-30
Primary meaning Leo
Literal translation lion
Edited by
Leo zodiac sign

सिंहस्तु शैले हृदयप्रदेशः प्रजापतेः पञ्चमम् आहुर् आद्याः।

siṃhas tu śaile hṛdayapradeśaḥ prajāpateḥ pañcamam āhur ādyāḥ|

‘The Lion (Leo) on the mountain is the heart-area of Prajāpati. The ancients called it the fifth sign.’

— Vṛddhayavanajātaka 1.8ab

Genre: astrological treatise
Provenance: India
Date: ca. 300–325 CE

Mak, Bill M. 2018. “The First Two Chapters of Mīnarāja’s Vṛddhayavanajātaka.” Zinbun 48: 1–31.
