𓁶𓊪𓂝𓂉𓈖𓏏𓏏𓇼
tpy-ꜥ ḫnt.t

Egyptian noun

Last edited: 2025-01-20
Primary meaning decan 10 (Senmut; Seti I A; Seti I C); decan 11 (Seti I B; Tanis; Ramses VI E)
Literal translation First one of the Khentet

𓁶𓊪𓂝𓂉𓈖𓏏𓏏𓇼

tpy-ꜥ ḫnt.t

‘First one of the Khentet’

— Neugebauer

Genre: tombs and temples
Provenance: Thebes; Roda, Hermopolis, Abydos, Esna, Edfu, Philae, Dendera, Tanis
Date: ca. 1200 BCE

Neugebauer, O. & Parker, R.A. EAT 3 108; 120; 130; 136; 143; 150
